pods:<hostname>Hash: hostname, address, bootId, reportedAt, cpuPercent, memoryBytes, memoryLimitBytes, sandboxes, sandboxSlots, sessionsPOST /workers/register (hostname, address); the worker's stats reporter every 10 s (all fields)30 s after the last write. Deleted when the worker shuts down.
logs:<hostname>Stream: one entry per log line, field line, the JSON line the worker writes to stdoutThe worker's log stream, every 1 s or 200 linesCapped at about LOG_MAX_LINES (10,000) entries; expires LOG_TTL_HOURS (24) after the last flush.

The server calls a worker only at the address in its pods:<hostname> key: a worker whose key has expired or been deleted is not called. The machines page shows a worker live while reportedAt is under 30 s old. See Stats reporter and Logging.
